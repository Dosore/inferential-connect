Disconnect QuickBooks Online

Revoke access in QuickBooks

Sign in to the relevant QuickBooks Online company with permission to manage connected apps. Open the connected-app management area, select the app you authorised for Inferential Limited and use its Disconnect or Remove option. Menu labels may vary. Confirm that you have selected the correct app and company.

Remove the local connection

Contact Denisosore@outlook.com to request removal of the company's ShortcutXL MCP configuration and locally stored authorisation tokens. State the company name, but do not send credentials, tokens or financial records. Identity and authority will need to be verified.

What disconnection does not do

  • It does not undo changes already made to company records.
  • It does not delete the QuickBooks company.
  • It does not automatically delete exported spreadsheets, local session history, logs or information retained by service providers.

Include any data-removal request when contacting Inferential Limited. See the privacy policy for the scope and limits of removal.
