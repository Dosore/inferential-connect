Disconnect QuickBooks Online

This page provides instructions for authorised internal operators. Visiting it does not automatically revoke access.

1. Revoke the company's authorisation

Sign in to the correct QuickBooks Online company with permission to manage connected apps. In its connected-app management area, find the app authorised for this integration and choose Disconnect or Remove. Labels may vary. Confirm both the company and the app before proceeding. This revokes the connection without deleting the QuickBooks company.

2. Remove the local connection

Ask the authorised operator to stop the matching MCP connection, remove its ShortcutXL configuration and secure credential entries, and remove its local authorisation-token file. Do not remove another company's connection or the sandbox by mistake. For help, email Denisosore@outlook.com without including credentials or financial records.

3. Review retained information

Disconnection does not undo transactions or automatically delete exported workbooks, session history, diagnostic records, backups or information held by service providers. Review these separately under applicable retention obligations and the privacy policy. Local deletion does not guarantee removal from every backup or provider system.
