Inferential Limited

An assisted QuickBooks Online integration for accounting analysis and spreadsheet-based work.

Inferential Limited, Uganda, is preparing an integration that allows an authorised operator to work with a connected QuickBooks Online company through a local MCP connector and ShortcutXL. Depending on permissions and instructions, this can include reading records and reports, and creating, updating or deleting supported records.

QuickBooks company records and credentials are not published on this website. This site provides information and instructions; it is not an accounting dashboard or a self-service authorisation service.

Before connecting

Each company must be authorised separately by a person with the necessary permissions. Sandbox and live company connections must be kept separate. Changes made to a live company affect that company's books.
