Private QuickBooks Online integration for Inferential Limited's own company.

This integration supports internal accounting, financial analysis and Excel-based work through a local QuickBooks MCP connector and ShortcutXL. It is operated by authorised personnel of Inferential Limited in Uganda. It is not currently offered for clients' companies or as a public service.

Authorised tasks may include reading financial reports and creating, updating, deleting, voiding or making supported records inactive. All live-company changes affect the actual company records, even if entered for testing.

Production access

Production onboarding is subject to Intuit's requirements. Publishing these pages does not grant production access. The public website contains information and assisted-operation instructions only. It does not display accounting records, store tokens or provide an automatic connection service.
