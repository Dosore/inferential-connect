Privacy policy

Prepared: 6 November 2026

Who operates the integration

Inferential Limited, Uganda, operates this assisted QuickBooks Online integration. Questions, requests and complaints can be sent to Denisosore@outlook.com. For client company records, the client's and Inferential Limited's respective data-protection roles and instructions must be agreed before processing begins.

Information the integration can access

Following company authorisation, the connector can access company identifiers and settings, financial reports, accounts, customers, suppliers, products, transactions and other records supported by the tools and QuickBooks permissions. Those records may contain names, contact details, balances, transaction descriptions and other personal or commercial information. Authorisation tokens and app credentials are also needed to maintain the connection. Capability to access a record does not mean every record is retrieved on every request.

Why information is processed

Information is processed to perform requested accounting and spreadsheet tasks, analyse financial information, carry out authorised record changes, maintain access and investigate operational problems. Before processing client data, Inferential Limited must establish the appropriate legal basis and obtain any required instructions or agreements. QuickBooks authorisation by itself is not consent from every individual named in company records.

Where information is processed and who receives it

The MCP connector runs on the operator's computer and communicates with Intuit's QuickBooks Online services. Returned tool results may enter the ShortcutXL session and may be sent to the AI model or other service configured for that session. Records may also be written into Excel workbooks, local files or session logs. Do not assume that a local connector means all data remains on the computer.

Intuit, ShortcutXL's service operators and any configured AI or other service providers may process information according to their own arrangements. Before live client use, Inferential Limited must identify the actual providers, their data-use and retention settings, and any international transfers involved, and agree appropriate protections with the client. This draft does not assert that these services have zero retention or exclude data from model training.

Credentials and access

The current setup uses ShortcutXL's operating-system credential store for app secrets. The local connector also stores company authorisation tokens in a local token file so it can refresh access. These files and the computer require appropriate access controls. Credentials and tokens must not be published on this website or placed in a public repository. No system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention and removal

Connection tokens remain locally until removed or replaced; revoking access in QuickBooks does not itself remove the local files. Exported workbooks, session history and diagnostic records may remain separately until deleted under the applicable retention arrangements. Before live client use, Inferential Limited must agree retention periods and deletion procedures, including applicable legal obligations and third-party service retention. This draft does not promise automatic deletion after disconnection.

Your choices and requests

You can revoke future app access through QuickBooks Online's connected-app controls. Contact Inferential Limited to request removal of the local connection and to ask about access, correction or deletion of information it holds. Requests will be assessed subject to applicable law, identity verification, client instructions and legitimate retention obligations. You may also complain to the competent data-protection authority where applicable.

This public website

The proposed information site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no account login, financial-data upload form or added analytics script. GitHub may process technical visit information under its own privacy statement. Do not send credentials or full financial records by ordinary email; use email to arrange an appropriate transfer method.

Changes

The final published policy will state its effective date. Material changes to processing arrangements will be communicated to affected clients.
