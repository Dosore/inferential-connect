Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026. This policy takes effect when published by Inferential Limited.

Scope and contact

Inferential Limited, Uganda, operates this integration solely for its own QuickBooks Online company and internal accounting work. It is not currently a service for client companies. Questions and data-protection requests may be sent to Denisosore@outlook.com. This policy concerns information handled through the integration, including personal information in company records.

Information and purposes

Authorised tool requests can retrieve company settings, accounts, financial reports, customers, suppliers, products, transactions and other supported records. These may include names, contact details, amounts, descriptions and employee-related information. The connector also uses app credentials, a company identifier and authorisation tokens. Information is used for requested accounting operations, financial analysis, spreadsheet work, connection maintenance and troubleshooting. The operator should request only information needed for the task.

Processing of personal information must have an appropriate basis under applicable law, such as fulfilling contractual or legal obligations or another lawful business purpose where applicable. Authorising QuickBooks access is not consent from every individual named in its records.

Actual data flow

The MCP connector runs on the operator's computer and connects to Intuit's QuickBooks Online API. Returned results can become part of ShortcutXL conversations and can be transmitted through Shortcut's cloud service to the configured AI model providers. The installed default configuration uses the Shortcut provider; models and routing can change between sessions. The connector running locally does not mean all processing remains local.

Information can also appear in Excel workbooks, exported files, local session history and diagnostic records. This public website does not contain financial records, request QuickBooks credentials or handle OAuth tokens.

Service providers and international processing

Services involved include Intuit, Shortcut (whose published privacy policy identifies Fundamental Research Labs), the AI and hosting providers used by Shortcut, and Microsoft where Excel or enabled cloud-file services are used. GitHub hosts this public information website and may receive technical visit information. These providers process information under their own terms and arrangements.

Shortcut's privacy policy, effective 20 October 2026, states that its data processing currently occurs in US data centres and that AI model API providers may retain calls for up to 30 days for safety monitoring. These are provider statements, not an independently verified guarantee of this account's routing or retention. Transfers outside Uganda may therefore occur. Applicable data-protection and transfer requirements must be considered before sending personal information.

AI model improvement

Shortcut's published policy says individual Pro and Teams accounts may have a setting called “Help improve Shortcut”, enabled by default, that permits conversation and related-artifact retention for model and product improvement and sharing with improvement partners. It says Enterprise data is not used for model training. The setting and plan for this installation have not been independently verified. Do not assume the exclusion for Google Workspace API data also applies to QuickBooks records.

For this internal accounting workflow, authorised operators are instructed to turn off “Help improve Shortcut” before starting a new conversation containing live company information. Shortcut states that turning it off affects new conversations, not necessarily existing ones. This policy does not assert that the setting has already been disabled or that a zero-retention agreement exists.

Storage, access and retention

App secrets are supplied through ShortcutXL's operating-system credential storage. The current connector also stores company refresh tokens and the company identifier in a local token file to maintain access. Local token files are not represented here as encrypted. Access to the computer, files and backups must be restricted appropriately. Tokens must not be placed on GitHub or this website.

Local session history, exported workbooks and diagnostic files can remain after the connection is removed. The integration has no verified automatic deletion schedule. Inferential Limited retains business records as needed for business and applicable legal obligations, and must assess removal of unnecessary copies. Service-provider retention is governed separately; a 30-day AI API retention statement is not a promise that all Shortcut sessions or local copies disappear within 30 days.

Disconnection and requests

Revoke future access in QuickBooks and arrange removal of local connection tokens as described on the disconnect page. This does not reverse posted transactions or erase information already exported or processed by providers. Contact Inferential Limited for access, correction, deletion, objection or other applicable data-protection requests. Requests may require identity verification and are subject to legal obligations and applicable rights. A request about provider-held information may also require contacting that provider.

Website and policy changes

This static website has no added analytics script, login or financial-data upload form. GitHub's hosting-related collection is governed by its policy. Do not email financial records or credentials; email only to arrange an appropriate process. Updates to this policy will be dated and material changes brought to internal users' attention.
