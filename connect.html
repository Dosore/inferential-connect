Connect or reconnect QuickBooks Online

This integration is operated through a local ShortcutXL environment. Company connections are assisted, not self-service on this website.

  1. Contact Denisosore@outlook.com to arrange onboarding. Do not email credentials or financial records.
  2. Review the terms and privacy policy, and agree the intended company, permitted work and data-handling arrangements.
  3. The operator prepares a separate credential and token configuration for the company. Sandbox and production environments must not share tokens.
  4. When a supported authorisation flow has been configured, a person authorised to connect the company signs in on Intuit's website, selects the correct company and approves access.
  5. The operator verifies the company identity with a read-only request before making any requested changes.

Reconnecting

If access has expired or been revoked, contact the operator to arrange a new authorisation. Do not share a browser callback URL, authorisation code or token by email or chat.

A production authorisation callback and operational connection flow still need to be configured. Merely publishing this page does not enable live access or establish that Intuit's production requirements are met.
