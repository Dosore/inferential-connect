Using the integration

Inferential Limited's integration runs in its authorised local ShortcutXL terminal and Excel environment. This website is an information page, not a hosted accounting dashboard.

  1. After successful authorisation through Intuit, return to the authorised ShortcutXL environment.
  2. Confirm that the intended company's MCP connection is loaded. Restart ShortcutXL after a new MCP configuration is installed.
  3. Retrieve company information to confirm the company identity before reading financial reports or requesting changes.
  4. Review the exact company, records and intended effect before instructing a live transaction change.

Opening this page alone does not mean the company is connected. Live access remains subject to Intuit's production requirements and valid company authorisation.
